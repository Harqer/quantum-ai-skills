# Research basis

1. Granet & Dreyer, "Benchmarking a heuristic Floquet adiabatic algorithm for the Max-Cut problem", arXiv:2404.16001. It fixes a finite Trotter step for classical target Hamiltonians and reports several-orders-of-magnitude gate-count reduction relative to ordinary continuous-time/Trotter scaling.
2. Quantinuum, "Theoretical work finds shortcut to solving the Max-Cut problem with a quantum computer", provider summary of the same result.

Transfer rule: this supports an algorithmic replacement for the stated optimization problem; it does not establish exact circuit equivalence and must not be transferred to exact SHA-256 semantics.
