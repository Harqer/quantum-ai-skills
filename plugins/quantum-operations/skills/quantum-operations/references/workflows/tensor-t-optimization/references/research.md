# Research basis

1. Ruiz et al., "Quantum circuit optimization with AlphaTensor", Nature Machine Intelligence 7, 374-385 (2025): T-count as symmetric tensor decomposition; arithmetic benchmarks; gadgetization; Karatsuba-like multiplication discovery.
2. https://github.com/google-deepmind/alphatensor_quantum — official RL/demo code and published decompositions.
3. https://github.com/tlaakkonen/circuit-to-tensor — OpenQASM 2 Clifford+T -> tensors, factorization -> circuit, QuiZX preoptimization, feynver verification, CS/CCZ gadgetization.

Re-read both repositories' current README/API before production commands.
